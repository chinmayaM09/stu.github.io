About Us

We help you help yourself

We are a group of people who take pride in supporting others to achieve their long-cherished dreams and goals.

Swadhaya was conceived and established in 2026. We believe in “swadhaya” — the meaning is “self study”. We want everyone to perform swadhaya and improve themselves. Self help is the best help.

We don’t offer any type of coaching or tuition. Rather, we offer students a variety of packages which comprise questions from every chapter of their syllabus, which they practise themselves to make themselves perfect.

How to get started

  1. Tell us what you want to practise Go to the Contact Us section and mention the chapter name along with the subject you wish to practise.
  2. We share the exam link As soon as we receive your request, we send you the exam link for that chapter.
  3. Practise on your own Attempt the question set at your own pace, then review your answers and improve.
  4. Finish with the mock tests Complete three mock tests covering all chapters of the semester to check your readiness.

Please note: chapter names should be as per CBSE / NCERT. Just reach the Contact Us section and mention the chapter name with the subject you wish to practise — we’ll share the exam link with you.
